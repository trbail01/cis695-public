## LinkedIn

### Announcement Post

NECC is hosting **Hack Your Room** — a one-day, hands-on technology camp for high school students in grades 8–12 across four Nebraska campuses this summer.

Lights that change when you walk in. Sensors that react. Automations that make life easier. Participants will "hack" their living space using Raspberry Pis, Home Assistant, and IoT devices — learning Linux and networking basics to run everything locally and securely. No prior experience is required.

This outreach initiative is designed to give students a practical preview of what computing and information technology education can look like — and to help them see themselves in those fields.

**Camp dates and locations:**
- West Point — June 18, 2026
- O'Neill — June 24, 2026
- South Sioux City — July 9, 2026
- Norfolk — July 16, 2026

Enrollment is capped at 15 students per location.

If you work with high school students or know families who might be interested, please share this opportunity.

🔗 Registration: [https://northeast.edu/campus-visit/summer-camps]
